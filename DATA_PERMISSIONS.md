# Data permissions

**Funeral Consumers Alliance of Central Texas (FCACTX).** FCACTX has given written authorization for academic use, establishment-level publication, and public redistribution of its survey publications and of the establishment-identifying data derived from them. The publications in `austin_pipeline/raw_fcactx_surveys/` remain © FCACTX. They are redistributed here with permission, as disclosure-safe copies with unchanged numeric content. Establishment names and business telephone numbers are business identifiers that appear in the published surveys. No private individual's contact information is included.

**Funeral Consumers Alliance of Greater Kansas City (FCA-GKC).** FCA-GKC has authorized publication and redistribution of its survey data. This release contains aggregate comparison files. The establishment-level transcription and the survey publications will be added.

**No other sources.** The repository contains no data from People's Memorial Association (Washington), the Cleveland Memorial Society, the FCA of the Finger Lakes, FCA Princeton/New Jersey, or any other organization. The paper cites the FCA of the Finger Lakes' published 2025 survey only as a reference.

**Use.** Please credit FCACTX and FCA-GKC as the data sources. The MIT License in `LICENSE` covers the code only; it does not relicense the survey publications. For uses of the survey publications beyond research replication, contact the issuing organization.
